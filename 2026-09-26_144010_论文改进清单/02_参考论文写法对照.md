## 二、逐篇写作结构剖析

### [4] Dorling et al., IEEE TSMC-S 2017 — "模型先行 + 图文强绑定"的范本

```
I. INTRODUCTION → II. RELATED WORK
III. MULTIROTOR HELICOPTER ENERGY CONSUMPTION   ← 先立物理模型
   eq(1) 单旋翼悬停功率 P=T^{3/2}/√(2ρA)   ← 引直升机经典 [34] Leishman
   eq(2) n 旋翼版 → eq(3) 线性近似 p(m)=αm+β   ← α=46.7 W/kg, β=26.9 W
   Fig.1 拟合图 → 平均误差 3.1%   ← 并附失效边界：m 到 10 kg 时误差 12.8%
IV. THE DRONE DELIVERY PROBLEMS（Assumptions / Variables & Constraints / MILP）
V. SIMULATED ANNEALING IMPLEMENTATION
VI. RESULTS（实测 / MILP与SA性能 / 参数分析 / 复用效应 / 电池重量优化）
VII. CONCLUSION + 41 条参考文献（IEEE 编号制）+ 作者简介
```

- **摘要里就有定量结论**："minimum cost has an inverse exponential relationship with the delivery time limit"。
- **立论**：引言用 5 家机构（Amazon Prime Air、DHL Parcelcopter、Google Project Wing、UAE、Matternet）
  铺陈现实需求，再逐条点出 FSTSP/GVRP 为何不够用——**先讲别人做了什么，再讲为什么不够**。
- **推导标注**：每个近似都给**拟合优度 + 失效边界**；图注写明全部假设参数（n=6, ρ=1.204 kg/m³, A=0.2 m², W=1.5 kg）。
- **结果呈现**：以 Table 为主、Fig 为辅；**用具名图形去解释另一张图的趋势**（Fig.8a 解释 Fig.6）。
- **局限**：每条"不做 X"都写成「识别弱点 → 说明它会怎么错 → 说明为何仍可接受」（见 §三·E）。
- **参考文献 41 条**，含教科书（Leishman、Kellerer、Kleinberg & Tardos）、经典算法原文
  （Kirkpatrick 1983、Metropolis 1953）与实际数据来源。**方法学溯源完整**。

### [5] Zhang et al., TRD 2021 — "统一口径 + 公开歧义"的范本

期刊版章节（据同源学位论文映射）：
`1 Introduction` → `2 Classifications` → `3 Theoretical Models`（**3.1 Unified Notation** / 3.2 Integrated / 3.3 Component）
→ `4 Analysis and Results` → `5 Discussions and Insights` → `6 Conclusions` + 三个附录

- **摘要**：无背景铺陈，直接"critical constraint → provides a uniform framework → we review/classify/assess
  → we document variations → results show great care must be taken"。
  **结论落在"方法论警示"而非"我们赢了"。**
- **口径统一（最强手法）**：**Table 3.2 统一符号表**（约 25 个符号，逐项标单位），
  并在表前**先声明歧义再取定**（见 §三·A）。
- **结果**：Table 3.1（12 模型分类矩阵）、3.3（baseline）、3.4（风/航电/完整剖面的修正，**逐行给 % 变化**）、
  3.5（LD/RH/R2/R3/LR 五条路径）；**4 组配对图 × 2 类平台 = 8 张图**。
- **读图三段式**（见 §三·C）。
- **局限/讨论讲到机理层**：integrated 模型 "very sensitive to the choice of the lift-to-drag ratio
  **and do not vary with airspeed**"；hovering-only 模型 "may provide very good approximations at low airspeeds.
  **However, at higher speeds they cannot capture the increased parasite drag that grows to dominate**"。
- **结论显式限定目标**："**Our goal is not to identify one preferred model, but rather to document the
  discrepancies between models and highlight the need for accurate calibration of whatever model is adopted.**"

### [6] ITU-R P.525-5 (11/2024) — 标准体范本

```
封面 + Foreword + Series 列表
Scope:    "This Recommendation provides methods to calculate the attenuation in free space."
Keywords: "Free space, attenuation, telecommunication links"
Related:  P.341, P.368 + NOTE "the latest edition in force should be used"
正文:     "The ITU Radiocommunication Assembly, considering … recommends that the methods in the Annex should be used"
Annex:  1 Introduction → 2 Basic formulae（2.1 point-to-area / 2.2 plane wave / 2.3 point-to-point）→ 3 Radar
```

- **每式紧跟符号与单位表**：式(6) 后直接写 `f : frequency (MHz) / d : distance (km)`。
- **ITU 官方常数为 `32.4`**。（*✅ 已从标准原文 PDF 独立复现*）

### [7] TI SLAA287B — 手册体，**只能引其事实陈述，不能引其方法论**

`ABSTRACT → TOC → 1 Introduction（三种电池化学对比 / C-rate 定义 / 三段式充电）→ 2 Measurement Circuit
→ 3 Software（Main / ADC ISR / Watchdog ISR / Flowchart）→ 4 Conclusion → 5 References → 6 Revision History`

**关键**：全文讲的是**电路与固件实现**，**没有 SOC–时间曲线，没有 65/35，没有 90% 拐点**。
*（✅ 已独立复现：`0.65 / 0.35 / 65% / 35% / 90% / SOC` 出现次数全部为 0。）*
可引的部分是事实句："Li-ion batteries **do not suffer from memory effect and do not need to be fully
discharged** before recharging"。

### [8] Zeng, Zhang & Lim, IEEE Comm. Mag. 2016 — 综述型 magazine，靠定量对比图说话

`I. INTRODUCTION（三类用例）→ II. BASIC NETWORKING ARCHITECTURE → III. CHANNEL CHARACTERISTICS
→ IV. MAIN DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS（Path Planning / Energy-Aware Deployment / MIMO）
→ V. COMMUNICATIONS WITH UAV CONTROLLED MOBILITY（Mobile Relaying / D2D Dissemination）→ VI. CONCLUSIONS`

- **摘要末句是"方法总纲句"的典范**："we provide an overview of UAV-aided wireless communications,
  **by introducing** the basic networking architecture and main channel characteristics,
  **highlighting** the key design considerations **as well as** the new opportunities to be exploited."
- **无公式推导、无链路预算方程**，靠 Fig.3(b) 这类定量对比图说话
  （"the path loss remains at a constant value that is about **14 dB lower** than that of the static relaying"）。
- **对本题目真正相关的三句原文**：
  1. "…due to e.g., **severe shadowing by urban or mountainous terrain, or damage to the communication
     infrastructure caused by natural disasters**"（正是本题场景）
  2. "**While LoS links are expected for such channels in most scenarios, they could also be occasionally
     blocked by obstacles such as terrain, buildings**, or the airframe itself."
  3. "**While increasing UAV altitude will lead to higher free space path loss, it also increases the
     possibility of having LoS links** with the ground terminals."（正是问题三"悬停高度"的核心张力）
- 补充：[8] 给的丘陵地形 Rician K 因子是 15 dB(L 波段)/28 dB(C 波段)，**不是衰落裕量**，
  论文的 `M_b = 8 dB` 不宜引 [8] 辩护。

## 三、可迁移的写作手法（带例证）

### A. 「统一符号表 + 先声明歧义再取定」— [5]（**直接命中论文的 A-1**）

[5] §3.3.1 原文："Different authors use different notation for the same concepts, so to facilitate
understanding and comparison of the models, we employ the unified notation shown in Table 3.2."
更关键的是它**把歧义摊开**："We use *l* to denote the drone flight range as the maximum distance that a
drone can travel in one direction and still be able to return to the depot, i.e., half of the round-trip
distance… **D'Andrea (2014) and Xu (2017) use the round-trip distance to denote the maximum range, while
Figliozzi (2017) does not specify how the range is defined.**"

→ 论文 A-1 已在做同样的事（"附录 2 给航程但未给巡航功率；此读法使 E_use 与 L_g 唯一相容"）并已量化 45.3%，
**只差把"谁怎么定义、文献惯例是什么"写出来**。

### B. 「公共设置对照实验 + 配对图」— [5]

原文："To assess the drone energy consumption models from the literature on a **common basis**, we evaluate
five fundamental modeling approaches … using **common drone design parameters and a common operational
setting**, where the payload and speed are allowed to vary."

→ 论文 §6.1 的 OFAT 灵敏度表是同型实验却**只用表格、未配图**，也**没把 A-1 的另一读法做成并列曲线**。
论文已有全部数据，只差两张图。

### C. 「读图三段式」— [5]

模板：① 陈述现象 → ② 给区间与百分比 → ③ 点极值与倍数。
原文："Figure 3.5 shows … **As expected, the flight range decreases for all models as payload increases.**
The range decreases vary from 1.3 km (11%) for LD to 0.6 km (17%) for R3 when moving from no payload to
0.5 kg. **The LD model provides the largest range of 10-12 km, and the other four models produce smaller
ranges between 3-6 km, which are 28-50% of the range of the LD model.**"

### D. 「数值结论后紧跟因果解释」— [4]

"It shows that, on average, the power consumed when performing the three manoeuvres is approximately equal.
The current is slightly lower during level flight, **possibly due to translational lift, where air moving
horizontally along the rotors creates extra lift**."
及 "Fig. 6 shows an inverse exponential relationship between c and T… **from Fig. 8a we can see that this
change comes mostly from adding or removing drones**."

→ 论文 §4.2.4 已有"由 18 → 23 架次，能耗仅降 1.59%，作业时间却增 24.9%，边际代价 3000–17000 s/kWh"
这样的强因果量化，**只缺图来"看出"转折来源**。

### E. 把「我们不做 X」写成「识别弱点 → 说明会怎么错 → 说明为何仍可接受」— [4]

原文："**Note that one weakness of this assumption is that it ignores the impact of weather.** Flying with
the wind, for example, could reduce energy consumption. Cold temperatures may adversely affect battery
performance until the batteries warm up. For the sake of generality, we ignore the effects of weather."
并**给失效边界**："if m varies between 0 kg and 10 kg, the mean percentage error becomes 12.8%"（对比 0–3 kg 时 3.1%）。

→ 论文 S-1 只有一句"强风会使航程显著下降"，缺量化边界。而 **[5] 恰有现成数字**：
强风可使能耗 **上升 129%~261%**。**补上就同时解决了"局限缺量化"和"[5] 无处可引"两个问题。**

### F. 贡献清单用「可核查动词」— [5]；摘要用一句「方法总纲」— [8]

[5] §3.1 末：(1) "review and classify … using a unified notation"; (2) "examine how the distinguishing
features … contribute to differences"; (3) "evaluate published results … from both the models and field
tests"; (4) "compare … in a common setting for two prototypical drones"。

→ 论文摘要是纯结果堆砌（每段一个问题的数字），**缺一句"本文做了什么"的骨架句**；
§7.1 的四条优点可改写成 [5] 式的可核查贡献。

### G. 结论开头显式限定目标 — [5]

"**Our goal is not to identify one preferred model, but rather to document the discrepancies between models
and highlight the need for accurate calibration of whatever model is adopted.**"

→ 论文 §7.4 的诚实性声明气质完全一致（"登记为不可判定项，在此显式声明，不伪装解决"），
但缺这样一句**目标声明**把语气从"证明我对"转向"界定不确定性边界"。

### H. 标准体：Scope → Keywords → Related → 每式紧跟符号与单位 — [6]

→ 论文 §4.1.4 `t_chg(s)` 分段函数、§4.1.5 `L_max^{a→b}` 等**未就地给单位**，读者须回翻第三章符号表。

### I. 手册类文献只引其「事实陈述」，不引其「方法论」— [7]

→ 论文若引 [7]，正确位置是"**电池可随时补电、无需深度放电**"（为"任务间补电"的建模合法性背书），
而不是"两阶段 SOC 线性充电"。

### J. 综述型文献也要给量化对比 — [8]

"for sufficiently large UAV maximum speed, e.g., v = 100 m/s, the UAV would be able to stay stationary
above the source and destination each for about 10 seconds, during which **the path loss remains at a
constant value that is about 14 dB lower than that of the static relaying**."
